Shows that when parameters are estimated from the data, the PIT values are no longer uniform under the null hypothesis. The deviation from uniformity depends on the number of estimated parameters and the sample size.

**Why cite:** Classic result showing that estimated parameters bias the PIT away from uniformity — relevant for understanding the limitations of PIT-based calibration diagnostics when parameters are fitted from the same data.
