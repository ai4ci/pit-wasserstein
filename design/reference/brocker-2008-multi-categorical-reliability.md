Reliability analysis of multi-categorical forecasts using a generalisation of the probability integral transform. Shows how reliability diagrams can be extended to arbitrary partitionings of the prediction space and connects them to the continuous PIT.

**Why cite:** Multi-categorical reliability framework that underlies the PIT histogram (a specific partitioning into equal-width bins), providing the theoretical basis for visual reliability diagnostics.
