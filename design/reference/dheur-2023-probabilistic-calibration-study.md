Dheur & Ben Taieb (2023), ICML 2023.

Large empirical study using PCE across 400+ configurations, standardising how
calibration is evaluated in neural-network regression. Uses Kuleshov's PCE
definition rather than proposing a new metric.

**Why cite:** the large-scale PCE study, i.e. evidence that PCE is the
incumbent.
