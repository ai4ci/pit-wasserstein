Hamill (2001), MWR 129:550-560.

Origin of rank histograms, the discrete counterpart of PIT histograms, for
ensemble weather forecasts. Shows that a flat histogram is necessary but not
sufficient for reliability: a flat histogram can come from an unreliable
ensemble, and a U shape can indicate conditional bias rather than
underdispersion.

**Why cite:** PIT histogram origins, and the warning that histogram shape alone
is not a metric.
