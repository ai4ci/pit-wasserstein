Wilks (2018), QJRMS 144:76-88.

First to propose penalising PIT deviation from uniform as part of a loss
function. Uses a binned chi-squared style measure rather than a Wasserstein
distance. Conceptual precursor to both Wessel's MCB and $D^*_{pit}$.

**Why cite:** the first PIT-uniformity penalty.
