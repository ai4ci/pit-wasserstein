Chapter 20 of *Random Walk, Brownian Motion, and Martingales*. Defines the Brownian bridge (tied-down Brownian motion), derives its Gaussian distribution, and discusses the Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic. Useful background for the empirical quantile-process limit in the significance-testing derivation; it does not, by itself, establish the distribution of the integrated absolute bridge functional.
