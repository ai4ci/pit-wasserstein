Demonstrates that scoring rules can be computed on transformed scales of the forecast and outcome. Shows how log scores and other proper scoring rules can be evaluated after transforming forecasts and observations, and discusses the implications for forecast evaluation in epidemiological contexts. Provides a practical framework for evaluating forecasts where the natural scale for calibration assessment differs from the observed scale.

**Why cite:** Score evaluation on transformed scales — relevant for the log-link and PIT-space transformations discussed in the paper.
