Brockwell (2007), Statistics & Probability Letters 77:1473-1478.

Generalises Rosenblatt's transformation so it applies to arbitrary probability
models, formalising the PIT as a generalised residual. Useful for
goodness-of-fit testing, and the basis for the universal-residual bias measure
used alongside $D^*_{pit}$.
