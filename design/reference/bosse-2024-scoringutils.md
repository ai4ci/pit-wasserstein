Bosse, Gruson, Cori, van Leeuwen, Funk & Abbott (2024), arXiv:2205.07090.

The `scoringutils` package implements PIT diagnostics including the
Anderson-Darling test for uniformity, but provides no Wasserstein-based
calibration metric.

**Why cite:** the current standard R tooling, and the evidence that the metric
is absent from it.
