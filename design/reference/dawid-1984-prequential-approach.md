Dawid (1984), *JRSS A* 147(2):278–290.

The prequential principle: a forecasting system should be judged by the
sequence of probability forecasts it issued and the outcomes that followed,
with no appeal to the model that generated them. Dawid states the calibration
criterion for sequential probability forecasts, which for continuous outcomes
is the uniformity of the PIT.

Gneiting & Resin (2023) attribute the probabilistic-calibration criterion
exactly this way: "suggested by Dawid (1984) and popularized by Diebold et al.
(1998), who proposed the use of PIT histograms as a diagnostic tool".
