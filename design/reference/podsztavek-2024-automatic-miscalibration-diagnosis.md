Podsztavek, Jordan, Tvrdík & Polsterer (2024), ESANN 2024:137-142.

Classifies PIT histogram shapes (U-shaped, dome, skewed) with machine learning:
automatic pattern recognition rather than a unified quantitative metric. The
directional $U^*_{pit}$ captures the same U-versus-dome distinction as a single
signed number.
