Derives explicit expressions for the distribution and moments of the integral of the absolute value of Brownian motion, extending Kac's work. The paper concerns Brownian motion, not the Brownian bridge; do not cite it as direct support for the exact CDF of the bridge functional without checking a valid transformation or bridge-specific source.
