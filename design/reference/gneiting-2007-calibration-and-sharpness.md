Gneiting, Balabdaoui & Raftery (2007), JRSS-B 69:243-268.

The canonical paper establishing the PIT histogram as the primary diagnostic
for checking calibration. Distinguishes probabilistic, exceedance and marginal
calibration, and introduces the **maximise sharpness subject to calibration**
paradigm that the paper's separation of calibration from bias and sharpness
builds on.

**Why cite:** canonical PIT/calibration framework.
